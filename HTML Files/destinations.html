Palawan

Limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons and world-class reefs.

9 places
01
📍 Palawan

Puerto Princesa

Puerto Princesa is a popular destination in Palawan known for its natural attractions and beautiful surroundings. It is home to the Underground River, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and offers a peaceful island environment.

🌿 Nature 🚣 Underground River 📸 Scenic
02
📍 Puerto Princesa, Palawan

Honda Bay

Honda Bay is a favorite island-hopping spot near Puerto Princesa, with small islands, sandbars, and clear shallow water that are perfect for swimming and snorkeling.

⛵ Island Hopping 🤿 Snorkeling 🏖️ Sandbars
03
📍 Palawan

El Nido

El Nido is known for its beautiful islands, clear waters, lagoons, and limestone landscapes. It is a popular place for island exploration and outdoor activities.

🤿 Snorkeling 🛶 Lagoons ⛵ Island Hopping
04
📍 Busuanga, Palawan

Coron

Coron is famous for its turquoise lakes, rugged limestone islands, and World War II shipwrecks that attract divers from around the world. Kayangan Lake and Twin Lagoon are among its best-known sights.

🚢 Wreck Diving 💧 Lakes 🌋 Limestone
05
📍 San Vicente, Palawan

Port Barton

Port Barton is a relaxed seaside village with calm bays, nearby islands, and snorkeling spots. It is a quieter alternative for travelers who want to slow down.

🧘 Relaxing 🐠 Snorkeling 💦 Waterfalls
06
📍 Culion, Palawan

Culion

Culion is an island with a rich and meaningful history. Visitors can learn about its past at the Culion Museum and Archives and enjoy its quiet coves and scenic views.

🏛️ History 📚 Museum 🌅 Views
07
📍 Calauit, Palawan

Calauit Safari Park

Calauit Safari Park is a wildlife sanctuary where you can see giraffes, zebras, and native Calamian deer roaming freely across open grasslands.

🦒 Wildlife 🚙 Safari 📸 Photography
08
📍 Sulu Sea, Palawan

Tubbataha Reefs

Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Sulu Sea and one of the top dive destinations in the Philippines, with sharks, turtles, and rich coral life. It is open to divers only during a limited season.

🤿 Diving 🦈 Marine Life 🪸 Coral Reef
09
📍 Balabac, Palawan

Balabac

Balabac sits at the southern tip of Palawan and is known for its remote white-sand islands, sandbars, and rare wildlife such as the Philippine mouse-deer.

🏝️ Remote Islands 🏖️ Sandbars 🦌 Wildlife
🌊

Occidental Mindoro

Remote reefs, wild mountains and rare wildlife.

5 places
10
📍 Sablayan, Occidental Mindoro

Apo Reef

Apo Reef Natural Park is one of the largest coral reef systems in the Philippines. Divers and snorkelers come here to see sharks, sea turtles, and colorful marine life.

🤿 Diving 🐢 Sea Turtles 🪸 Coral Reef
11
📍 Sablayan, Occidental Mindoro

Sablayan

Sablayan is the gateway to Apo Reef. Nearby Pandan Island offers a calm place to swim, snorkel, and relax on the beach.

🏝️ Pandan Island 🏊 Swimming 🧘 Relaxing
12
📍 Mindoro

Mt. Iglit-Baco National Park

This mountain national park protects the habitat of the tamaraw, a rare dwarf buffalo found only in Mindoro. It is a place for nature lovers and wildlife conservation.

🦬 Tamaraw ⛰️ Mountains 🌲 Forest
13
📍 Lubang, Occidental Mindoro

Lubang Islands

The Lubang Islands offer quiet beaches, scenic coastlines, and World War II history, making them a good choice for travelers who enjoy off-the-beaten-path trips.

🏖️ Beaches 🏛️ History 🗺️ Off the Beaten Path
14
📍 Mamburao, Occidental Mindoro

Mamburao

Mamburao is the provincial capital of Occidental Mindoro. It is a good base for exploring the coast and the surrounding countryside.

🏙️ Capital Town 🌅 Sunsets 🍤 Local Food
🌴

Oriental Mindoro

Famous beaches, diving spots and mountain adventures.

4 places
15
📍 Oriental Mindoro

Puerto Galera

Puerto Galera is known for its beaches, coves, and some of the best diving and snorkeling in the country. It sits on the Verde Island Passage, a very biodiverse stretch of sea.

🏖️ White Beach 🤿 Diving 🐠 Marine Life
16
📍 Calapan, Oriental Mindoro

Calapan

Calapan is the provincial capital and a main entry point by sea. Visitors can learn about the Mangyan indigenous peoples of Mindoro and enjoy the city's local food.

🎨 Mangyan Culture ⛴️ Gateway 🍲 Local Food
17
📍 Oriental Mindoro

Mount Halcon

Mount Halcon is one of the highest peaks in the Philippines and a challenging trek for experienced hikers, with misty forests and dramatic mountain views.

🥾 Trekking ⛰️ Mountain ☁️ Views
18
📍 Naujan, Oriental Mindoro

Naujan Lake

Naujan Lake National Park is one of the largest lakes in the Philippines and an important home for migratory and resident birds.

🐦 Birdwatching 🛶 Lake 🌿 Nature
🎭

Marinduque

The heart-shaped island of festivals, faith and beaches.

5 places
19
📍 Boac, Marinduque

Boac

Boac is the capital of Marinduque and the heart of the famous Moriones Festival during Holy Week. Its hilltop cathedral and old streets show the island's rich culture.

🎭 Moriones ⛪ Cathedral 🏛️ History
20
📍 Gasan, Marinduque

Gasan

Gasan is a coastal town known for its beaches, its festival celebrations, and its closeness to the Tres Reyes Islands.

🏖️ Beaches 🎉 Festivals ⛵ Boat Trips
21
📍 Gasan, Marinduque

Tres Reyes Islands

The Tres Reyes Islands are three small islands named Gaspar, Melchor, and Baltazar. They are great for snorkeling, diving, and a quiet island day.

🏝️ Three Islands 🤿 Snorkeling 🛶 Boat Ride
22
📍 Torrijos, Marinduque

Poctoy White Beach

Poctoy White Beach is a long stretch of pale sand and calm water, popular for swimming, picnics, and sunsets.

🏖️ White Sand 🌇 Sunsets 🧺 Picnics
23
📍 Marinduque

Mt. Malindig

Mt. Malindig is a dormant volcano and the highest point on Marinduque, with trails that lead through forest to wide views of the island.

🌋 Volcano 🥾 Hiking 🌄 Views
⛰️

Romblon

The marble islands of beaches, forts and wild mountains.

7 places
24
📍 Romblon

Romblon

Romblon is known for its islands, beaches, natural scenery, and marble industry. Visitors can experience both its natural attractions and local culture, including the old Fort San Andres.

🗿 Marble 🏰 Fort San Andres 🎨 Culture
25
📍 Romblon Island

Bonbon Beach

Bonbon Beach is a scenic stretch of sand on Romblon Island, known for its calm water and beautiful views.

🏖️ Beach 🏊 Swimming 📸 Scenic
26
📍 Sibuyan Island, Romblon

Sibuyan Island

Sibuyan Island is often called the "Galapagos of Asia" for its rich wildlife. It is home to Mt. Guiting-Guiting and the clear waters of Cantingas River.

⛰️ Guiting-Guiting 💧 Cantingas River 🦎 Biodiversity
27
📍 Banton, Romblon

Banton Island

Banton Island is known for its history, including an ancient burial cave and the traditional Banton cloth. It also has quiet beaches and coves.

🏛️ History 🧵 Banton Cloth 🏝️ Coves
28
📍 Near Tablas, Romblon

Carabao Island

Carabao Island is a laid-back island with white beaches and clear water, a great place to unwind away from the crowds.

🏖️ White Beach 🧘 Relaxing 🐠 Snorkeling
29
📍 Romblon

Cobrador Island

Cobrador Island is a small island known for its white sand and peaceful atmosphere, perfect for a day trip by boat.

🏝️ Small Island 🛶 Day Trip 🌅 Quiet
30
📍 Tablas Island, Romblon

Tablas Island

Tablas is the largest island in Romblon, with towns like Odiongan, beaches, waterfalls, and countryside views.

🏞️ Countryside 💦 Waterfalls 🏘️ Island Towns
