Welcome

Welcome to MIMAROPA

MIMAROPA, also known as Region IV-B, is a region in the Philippines known for its beautiful islands, beaches, natural attractions, and rich culture. The name MIMAROPA comes from the provinces of Mindoro, Marinduque, Romblon, and Palawan.

The region offers many places to explore, from beautiful beaches and islands to historical and cultural attractions. MIMAROPA is a great destination for travelers who want to experience nature, adventure, food, and local traditions.
