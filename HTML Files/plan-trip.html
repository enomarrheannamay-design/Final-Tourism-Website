Transport

Getting There & Around

Islands mean planes, boats, and a little patience.

✈️

By Air

Palawan has airports at Puerto Princesa, El Nido, and Busuanga (for Coron). Romblon is also reached by air. Check airlines for current routes.

⛴️

By Ferry

Ferries and roll-on roll-off boats link Luzon ports, such as Batangas and Lucena, to Mindoro, Marinduque, and Romblon.

🚐

By Land

Vans, jeepneys, buses, and tricycles connect towns on each island. Long road trips can take several hours.

🛥️

By Boat Tour

Local outrigger boats take you island hopping. Book with a licensed, local operator and wear a life vest.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

When is the best time to visit MIMAROPA?

The drier months, roughly from December to May, usually bring calmer seas and clearer skies. The rainy season has more storms, and boat trips can be cancelled when the weather is bad.

Do I need to book in advance?

It is a good idea, especially for flights, ferries, and rooms during holidays, Holy Week, and the dry season when many people travel.

Is there mobile signal and internet on the islands?

Towns and popular areas usually have signal, but remote islands may have weak or no coverage. Download maps and booking details before you go.

Should I bring cash?

Yes. Many small shops, boat operators, and eateries accept cash only, and ATMs can be far from beaches and small islands.

Can I swim and snorkel anywhere?

Only in safe areas. Ask your guide or resort about currents and tides, wear a life vest if you are not a strong swimmer, and never touch the corals.
